# Lead Quality Improve Karne ke 7 Practical Ways for Meta & Google Ads

More leads do not always mean better growth. If your sales team is spending time on unqualified enquiries, the real goal is to improve lead quality and connect advertising data with business outcomes.

## 1. Define a qualified lead

Write down the minimum conditions that make an enquiry valuable: correct service need, reachable contact details, realistic budget, buying timeline and decision-making authority. Use the same definition across Meta Ads, Google Ads and your CRM.

## 2. Match the message to intent

Meta Ads can create demand, while Google Ads often captures active search intent. Create separate landing-page messages for each audience instead of sending every click to one generic page. The promise in the ad should match the first screen on the landing page.

## 3. Use stronger forms and questions

Ask one or two useful qualifying questions, such as service needed, monthly budget or preferred start date. Keep forms short, but collect enough information to separate genuine prospects from accidental or low-intent submissions.

## 4. Track campaigns correctly

Use consistent UTM values for source, medium and campaign. Track form submissions, WhatsApp conversations, booked calls and qualified leads separately. A click or raw enquiry is not the same as a sales opportunity.

## 5. Improve response speed

Route every new enquiry to an owner and set a clear response-time target. Fast, relevant replies reduce lead wastage. Record whether the lead replied, booked a call, became qualified or was marked irrelevant.

## 6. Send quality signals back to ad platforms

Review performance by qualified leads and revenue, not only cost per lead. Where possible, connect your CRM or offline conversion process so Meta and Google can learn which enquiries became real opportunities.

## 7. Review the funnel every week

Compare campaign, audience, keyword, device and landing-page performance. Pause sources that repeatedly create poor-fit enquiries, improve pages with strong traffic but weak conversion quality, and create new ads around the messages used by your best customers.

## Simple weekly scorecard

Track spend, clicks, enquiries, qualified leads, booked calls, sales, revenue and cost per qualified lead. This makes budget decisions clearer than relying on lead volume alone.
