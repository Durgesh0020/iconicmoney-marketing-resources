# Why Meta Ads Get Leads but No Sales

A low cost per lead is not the same as a sales-ready opportunity. When Meta Ads produce enquiries but few customers, review the complete journey: targeting, offer clarity, landing-page relevance, qualification, follow-up speed and conversion tracking.

## Practical checks

- Define a qualified lead using service fit, budget, location and timeline.
- Keep the ad promise and landing-page message aligned.
- Ask useful qualification questions without adding unnecessary friction.
- Track contacted leads, qualified leads, meetings, sales and revenue.
- Compare cost per qualified lead and cost per sale, not only cost per form.
